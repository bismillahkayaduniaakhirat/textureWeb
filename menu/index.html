TEXTURE

Feel the Pleasure in every Texture

Located in a historic corner of Kediri, Texture Coffee is a cozy place that beautifully blends tradition with modernity. Nestled within a building that echoes the city heritage, this cafe offers a unique ambiance where the past meets the present.

The menu is entirely homemade, crafted with love and expertise by local talent who bring a deep understanding of Nusantara's culinary roots to every dish and cup of coffee.
